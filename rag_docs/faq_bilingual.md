# Solar FAQs — Bilingual (English / Roman Urdu)

Educational reference only. Actual design, pricing, and installation advice should come from a qualified installer and your electricity distribution company.

### Q1 (EN): How much solar do I need for my home?
**A (EN):** Start with your average monthly units from recent electricity bills, then list the appliances you want to run during load-shedding. A system is sized from daily energy use, panel production, inverter capacity, and battery backup—not roof size alone. Lights, fans, fridge, TV, and chargers need far less capacity than regularly running an AC, water pump, or freezer. Build a realistic essential-load list in the app, then treat the result as a planning estimate and have an installer verify it on site.

### Q1 (Roman Urdu): Meray ghar ke liye kitna solar chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Pehlay apne recent bijli bills ka monthly average dekhein, phir decide karein ke load-shedding mein kaunsi cheezein chalani hain. Solar system sirf roof ke size se nahi hota; daily units, panels ki production, inverter capacity aur battery backup sab count hota hai. Lights, fans, fridge, TV aur chargers ke liye kam capacity chahiye hoti hai, lekin AC, water pump ya freezer regularly chalana ho to zyada lagti hai. App mein essential loads add kar ke estimate banayein, phir installer se site survey karwa kar final design confirm karwayein.

### Q2 (EN): Will solar eliminate my electricity bill?
**A (EN):** It can reduce the bill substantially, but it does not automatically make it zero. The result depends on system size, sunlight, household consumption, battery use, and whether net metering is approved. If surplus units are exported, net metering or net billing can offset imported units under the applicable rules. Fixed charges, taxes, fuel-price adjustments, and billing policies may still appear. Treat solar as a long-term cost-reduction investment, not a guaranteed zero-bill promise.

### Q2 (Roman Urdu): Kya solar se bijli ka bill zero ho jayega?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Bill bohat kam ho sakta hai, lekin zero hone ki guarantee nahi. Ye system size, sunlight, ghar ke consumption, battery use aur net metering approval par depend karta hai. Agar extra units grid ko export hote hain to net metering/net billing se import units offset ho sakte hain, lekin fixed charges, taxes aur fuel adjustment ab bhi bill mein aa sakte hain. Solar ko bill kam karne wali long-term investment samjhein, zero-bill guarantee nahi.

### Q3 (EN): What is the difference between kW and kWh?
**A (EN):** kW is power: how much electricity equipment uses or produces at one moment. kWh is energy: power used over time. A 1,000 W appliance running for one hour uses about 1 kWh. Panels are rated in kW, while bills and battery capacity are usually discussed in kWh. For planning, kW decides whether the inverter can run your appliances; kWh decides how long batteries can keep them running.

### Q3 (Roman Urdu): kW aur kWh mein kya farq hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** kW power hai, yani kisi waqt kitni electricity use ya produce ho rahi hai. kWh energy hai, yani waqt ke sath kitni electricity use hui. Misal ke tor par 1,000 W appliance aik hour chale to taqreeban 1 kWh use hota hai. Panels kW mein hote hain, jabke bill aur battery capacity aksar kWh mein hoti hai. Inverter appliances chala sakta hai ya nahi, ye kW se pata chalta hai; battery kitni der backup degi, ye kWh se pata chalta hai.

### Q4 (EN): How many panels do I need?
**A (EN):** It depends on panel wattage, usable roof space, shading, panel direction, and your energy target. A 550 W panel does not produce its full rating all day because of sunlight angle, heat, dust, clouds, and conversion losses. Do not choose panels only by roof area. First calculate the daily kWh you want solar to cover, then have a qualified installer design the panel array, inverter, and battery combination.

### Q4 (Roman Urdu): Mujhe kitne panels chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Ye panel wattage, roof space, shading, panel direction aur aap ke energy target par depend karta hai. 550 W panel din bhar full rating produce nahi karta kyunke sunlight angle, garmi, dust, clouds aur losses hote hain. Sirf roof dekh kar panels decide na karein. Pehlay calculate karein ke daily kitne kWh solar se cover karne hain, phir qualified installer se panels, inverter aur battery ka combined design banwayein.

### Q5 (EN): Is a bigger inverter always better?
**A (EN):** No. An oversized inverter can cost more and may not operate efficiently at low loads. It must handle your simultaneous running load and the surge from motors such as ACs, pumps, and refrigerators. It must also match the PV array and battery system. Choose an inverter based on actual load, essential backup circuits, and a realistic future-expansion plan—not simply the biggest model available.

### Q5 (Roman Urdu): Kya bara inverter hamesha behtar hota hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Nahi. Bara inverter mehnga ho sakta hai aur kam load par efficient nahi chal sakta. Inverter ko aap ke appliances ka simultaneous load aur AC, pump, fridge jaise motors ka surge handle karna hota hai. Panels aur battery ke sath bhi compatible hona chahiye. Inverter actual load, essential backup circuits aur future expansion ke hisaab se choose karein, sirf sab se bara model nahi.

### Q6 (EN): Can solar run my air conditioner?
**A (EN):** Yes, but it depends on AC size, inverter capacity, battery capacity, and whether it runs in daylight or at night. A 1-ton inverter AC commonly has a running range around 700–1,200 W, while a 1.5-ton may be around 1,000–1,800 W; compressor start can create a higher surge. Running an AC at night requires enough battery capacity and an inverter that can supply the surge. Do not assume one AC will run all night on a small battery.

### Q6 (Roman Urdu): Kya solar AC chala sakta hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Haan, lekin ye AC ke size, inverter capacity, battery capacity aur is baat par depend karta hai ke AC din ko chalega ya raat ko. 1-ton inverter AC ki running load taqreeban 700–1,200 W ho sakti hai, jabke 1.5-ton ki 1,000–1,800 W; compressor start par surge zyada hota hai. Raat ko AC chalane ke liye kaafi battery capacity aur suitable inverter chahiye. Chhoti battery se poora raat AC chalega, aisa assume na karein.

### Q7 (EN): How long will my batteries last?
**A (EN):** Battery life depends on chemistry, quality, temperature, depth of discharge, charging settings, and cycling frequency. Quality LiFePO4 batteries are generally designed for many more charge cycles than basic lead-acid batteries, but no battery lasts forever. Avoid keeping batteries at very low charge for long periods, exposing them to extreme heat, or using incompatible inverter settings. Ask the supplier for warranty terms, cycle rating, and recommended operating settings.

### Q7 (Roman Urdu): Meri battery kitni der chalegi?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Battery ki life chemistry, quality, garmi, depth of discharge, charging settings aur daily cycling par depend karti hai. Achi LiFePO4 battery aam tor par simple lead-acid se zyada charge cycles deti hai, lekin koi battery hamesha ke liye nahi hoti. Battery ko lambe arsay tak bohat low charge par na rakhein, extreme heat se bachayein, aur inverter settings compatible rakhein. Supplier se warranty, cycle rating aur recommended settings zaroor poochein.

### Q8 (EN): Can I install solar myself?
**A (EN):** You can do simple visual checks, but electrical installation should be done by a qualified solar installer or electrician. Grid-connected systems involve high-voltage DC, AC wiring, earthing, protection devices, and utility approval. Incorrect installation can cause fire, electric shock, equipment damage, and net-metering approval problems. Use a professional for design, installation, commissioning, and any repair involving wiring or the inverter.

### Q8 (Roman Urdu): Kya main khud solar install kar sakta hoon?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Simple visual check to kar sakte hain, lekin electrical installation qualified solar installer ya electrician se karwana behtar hai. Grid-connected system mein high-voltage DC, AC wiring, earthing, protection devices aur utility approval hoti hai. Ghalat installation se fire, electric shock, equipment damage ya net-metering approval mein masla ho sakta hai. Design, installation, commissioning aur wiring/inverter ki repair qualified professional se karwayein.

### Q9 (EN): What is the difference between on-grid, hybrid, and off-grid?
**A (EN):** An on-grid system mainly reduces daytime grid use and may export surplus power, but it usually does not provide backup during a power cut. A hybrid system includes batteries, so it can run selected appliances during load-shedding. An off-grid system works without the utility grid and needs enough panels, batteries, and usually a backup generator. For most Pakistani homes wanting both bill savings and outage backup, hybrid is the common choice.

### Q9 (Roman Urdu): On-grid, hybrid aur off-grid mein kya farq hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** On-grid system zyada tar din ke waqt grid use kam karta hai aur extra units export kar sakta hai, lekin load-shedding mein backup nahi deta. Hybrid system mein battery hoti hai, is liye load-shedding mein selected appliances chal sakte hain. Off-grid system grid ke baghair chalta hai aur is mein kaafi panels, batteries aur aksar backup generator chahiye hota hai. Pakistani ghar ke liye jo bill savings aur load-shedding backup dono chahte hain, hybrid aam tor par suitable option hai.

### Q10 (EN): What is net metering?
**A (EN):** Net metering is an arrangement with your electricity distribution company that allows an approved solar system to export surplus electricity to the grid. Exported units can offset imported units according to the current NEPRA rules and your DISCO's process. You generally need an approved application, compliant equipment, inspection, and a bi-directional meter. Do not assume every connection or installation automatically qualifies.

### Q10 (Roman Urdu): Net metering kya hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Net metering aik arrangement hai jis mein approved solar system extra electricity grid ko export kar sakta hai. Export hone wale units aap ke import units ko offset kar sakte hain, NEPRA rules aur DISCO ke process ke mutabiq. Is ke liye application approval, compliant equipment, inspection aur aam tor par bi-directional meter chahiye hota hai. Har connection ya har solar installation automatically qualify nahi hota.

### Q11 (EN): Which companies provide net metering in Pakistan?
**A (EN):** Net metering is generally available through distribution companies such as LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, GEPCO, FESCO, and other DISCOs, subject to their own application process and current regulations. Documents, inspection requirements, timelines, and technical conditions can differ. Before buying equipment, ask your installer to confirm the process for your specific DISCO and connection, and keep copies of all approvals and inspection documents.

### Q11 (Roman Urdu): Pakistan mein net metering kaunsi companies deti hain?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Net metering aam tor par LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, GEPCO, FESCO aur doosri DISCOs ke zariye available hoti hai, lekin process aur rules alag ho sakte hain. Documents, inspection, timeline aur technical requirements company ke hisaab se change hote hain. Equipment kharidne se pehle installer se apni specific DISCO aur connection ke liye process confirm karwayein, aur har approval/inspection document ki copy rakhein.

### Q12 (EN): Do I need batteries if I have net metering?
**A (EN):** Not necessarily. Net metering helps reduce the bill by offsetting imported units with exported surplus units, but it does not keep your home powered during an outage. If load-shedding backup matters, you need a hybrid inverter and batteries, or another backup arrangement. Some households install solar first and add batteries later, but the inverter and wiring must be designed to support that upgrade.

### Q12 (Roman Urdu): Kya net metering hone par battery zaroori hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Zaroori nahi. Net metering exported units se imported units offset kar ke bill kam kar sakti hai, lekin load-shedding mein ghar ko power nahi deti. Agar backup chahiye to hybrid inverter aur batteries chahiye, ya alag backup arrangement. Kuch log pehle solar lagwate hain aur baad mein battery add karte hain, lekin inverter aur wiring ko us upgrade ke liye design hona chahiye.

### Q13 (EN): Should I choose lithium or lead-acid batteries?
**A (EN):** LiFePO4 lithium batteries usually suit households that cycle batteries daily and want lower maintenance and longer service life. They cost more upfront. Lead-acid batteries can be a lower-initial-cost option, especially for limited backup needs, but flooded types need maintenance and all types have shorter practical life under heavy daily cycling. Match battery chemistry, voltage, communication, and charging settings to the inverter.

### Q13 (Roman Urdu): Lithium ya lead-acid battery kaunsi lein?
**A (Roman Urdu):** LiFePO4 lithium battery un ghar ke liye aam tor par behtar hai jo daily battery cycle karte hain aur kam maintenance aur longer life chahte hain; upfront cost zyada hoti hai. Lead-acid battery shuru mein sasti par sakti hai, especially agar backup limited hai, lekin flooded type ko maintenance chahiye aur heavy daily use mein practical life kam ho sakti hai. Battery chemistry, voltage, communication aur charging settings inverter ke sath match honi chahiye.

### Q14 (EN): How much battery capacity do I need?
**A (EN):** Calculate the appliances needed during an outage, their running watts, and the number of hours you want them to run. Add a margin for inverter losses, battery ageing, and days with less solar production. Do not size batteries only by the number of ACs you own. A realistic essential-load list is more useful than trying to back up the entire house.

### Q14 (Roman Urdu): Mujhe kitni battery capacity chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Pehlay decide karein ke load-shedding mein kaunse appliances chalane hain, unki running watts kya hain, aur kitne hours chahiye. Inverter losses, battery ageing aur kam sunlight wale dinon ke liye margin rakhein. Sirf is liye battery na barhayein ke aap ke paas AC zyada hain. Puri ghar ko backup dene ke bajaye essential loads ki realistic list zyada useful hoti hai.

### Q15 (EN): Can I add batteries later?

Not every inverter accepts additional batteries, and mixing old and new batteries or different chemistries can cause problems. Plan expansion with the original installer or a qualified professional before buying equipment. Keep records of battery model, capacity, voltage, communication protocol, and inverter settings.

### Q15 (Roman Urdu): Kya baad mein battery add kar sakte hain?

### Q16 (EN): How often should I clean solar panels?
**A (EN):** In dusty Pakistani cities, inspect panels monthly and clean them when dust, smog, leaves, or bird droppings are visible. Cleaning frequency depends on location, nearby construction, traffic, and weather. Clean early morning or evening using water and a soft brush or cloth. Never climb a wet roof, work during peak sun, or use high-pressure water near electrical equipment.

### Q16 (Roman Urdu): Solar panels kitni dafa saaf karne chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Dusty Pakistani cities mein panels ko monthly inspect karein aur jab dust, smog, pattay ya birds ki dropping nazar aaye to saaf karein. Cleaning ki frequency location, construction, traffic aur weather par depend karti hai. Subah ya shaam ko paani aur soft brush/cloth se saaf karein. Geelay roof par na chadhein, peak sun mein kaam na karein, aur electrical equipment ke qareeb high-pressure water use na karein.

### Q17 (EN): Do lead-acid batteries need water?
**A (EN):** Flooded lead-acid batteries may need distilled-water topping as part of routine maintenance, but only according to the manufacturer's instructions. Do not open sealed AGM or gel batteries, and never add tap water or acid unless the manufacturer specifically directs it. Keep terminals clean and tight, ensure ventilation, and have a professional inspect the battery bank if you notice swelling, leakage, heat, or a strong smell.

### Q17 (Roman Urdu): Kya lead-acid battery ko paani dena hota hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Flooded lead-acid battery ko manufacturer ki instructions ke mutabiq distilled water add karna parta hai, lekin sealed AGM ya gel battery ko kholein nahi. Tap water ya acid tabhi add karein jab manufacturer clearly bole. Terminals clean aur tight rakhein, ventilation ensure karein, aur agar battery swollen, leaking, hot ya strong smell de rahi ho to professional se inspect karwayein.

### Q18 (EN): What yearly maintenance does a solar system need?
**A (EN):** Arrange at least one annual inspection by a qualified technician. They should check panel mounting, wiring, connectors, earthing, breakers, battery terminals, inverter fans and vents, error logs, and charge settings. Homeowners can keep the inverter area clean and ventilated, monitor app alerts, and report repeated faults. Do not open the inverter or battery enclosure yourself.

### Q18 (Roman Urdu): Solar system ko saal mein kitni maintenance chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Saal mein aik dafa qualified technician se inspection karwayein. Technician ko panels mounting, wiring, connectors, earthing, breakers, battery terminals, inverter fans/vents, error logs aur charge settings check karne chahiye. Homeowner inverter area clean aur ventilated rakh sakta hai, app alerts dekh sakta hai, aur repeated faults report kar sakta hai. Inverter ya battery enclosure khud na kholein.

### Q19 (EN): My inverter shows an error code. What should I do?
**A (EN):** First photograph the display and note the exact model number and code. Check the error-code guide for your brand, but remember that the same code can mean different things on different models. Do safe checks only: confirm the grid breaker is on, ensure battery terminals are tight, reduce heavy loads, and restart only if the manual permits. If the fault returns, or there is burning smell, heat, tripping, or battery swelling, call a professional.

### Q19 (Roman Urdu): Mere inverter par error code aa raha hai, kya karoon?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Pehlay display ki photo lein aur exact model number aur code note karein. Apni brand ke error-code guide se dekhein, lekin yaad rakhein ke same code alag models mein alag matlab ho sakta hai. Sirf safe checks karein: grid breaker on hai ya nahi, battery terminals tight hain ya nahi, heavy loads kam karein, aur manual allow kare to restart karein. Agar fault wapis aaye, burning smell, heat, tripping ya battery swelling ho to professional ko call karein.

### Q20 (EN): Why does my inverter keep tripping?
**A (EN):** Repeated tripping can come from overload, a faulty appliance, loose wiring, battery problems, grid issues, or an internal inverter fault. Turn off non-essential loads and see whether the problem stops. Do not repeatedly reset a tripping breaker or bypass protection. If it trips again with only basic loads connected, or cables or the inverter are hot, stop using it and call a qualified installer.

### Q20 (Roman Urdu): Mera inverter baar baar trip kyun ho raha hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Baar baar trip hone ki wajah overload, kharab appliance, loose wiring, battery masla, grid issue ya inverter ki internal fault ho sakti hai. Non-essential loads off karein aur dekhein ke masla rukta hai ya nahi. Tripping breaker ko baar baar reset ya bypass na karein. Agar sirf basic loads laga kar bhi trip ho, ya cables/inverter garam hon, to use band karein aur qualified installer ko call karein.

### Q21 (EN): My solar production is low. What could be wrong?
**A (EN):** Common reasons include dust or shading on panels, cloudy weather, winter sun angles, smog, a panel/string fault, loose DC connections, or an inverter/monitoring issue. Check the app's daily production history and compare similar weather days. Look for new shading from trees, construction, water tanks, or antennas. If production stays low in clear weather, ask a technician to inspect the array and inverter.

### Q21 (Roman Urdu): Meri solar production kam kyun hai?

Net-metering approval may also need updating if system capacity changes. Ask the installer to design the initial system with a realistic future-expansion path.

### Q24 (EN): What happens during load-shedding?
**A (EN):** With a basic on-grid system, the inverter usually shuts down for safety and the house loses power. With a hybrid system, the inverter can supply selected essential circuits from batteries and, if sunlight is available, from panels. Which appliances stay on depends on inverter capacity, battery charge, and backup wiring design. Heavy loads such as ACs and pumps may need to be excluded from backup.

### Q24 (Roman Urdu): Load-shedding mein kya hota hai?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Basic on-grid system mein inverter safety ke liye shut down ho jata hai aur ghar ki power chali jati hai. Hybrid system mein inverter batteries se, aur agar sunlight ho to panels se bhi, selected essential circuits chala sakta hai. Kaunse appliances chalenge, ye inverter capacity, battery charge aur backup wiring design par depend karta hai. AC aur pump jaise heavy loads backup se exclude karne par sakte hain.

### Q25 (EN): Do I need a voltage stabilizer with solar?
**A (EN):** A stabilizer may help sensitive appliances during voltage fluctuations, but it does not replace correct solar design, earthing, surge protection, or grid approval. Ask your installer whether your inverter and appliances need one, based on local voltage conditions and equipment specifications. Never connect a stabilizer or generator in a way that bypasses approved protection or creates backfeed into the grid.

### Q25 (Roman Urdu): Kya solar ke sath voltage stabilizer chahiye?
**A (Roman Urdu):** Voltage fluctuation mein sensitive appliances ke liye stabilizer madadgar ho sakta hai, lekin ye correct solar design, earthing, surge protection ya grid approval ka replacement nahi. Installer se poochein ke aap ke inverter aur appliances ko stabilizer chahiye ya nahi, local voltage conditions aur equipment specifications ke hisaab se. Stabilizer ya generator aise connect na karein jo approved protection bypass kare ya grid mein backfeed create kare.

### Q26 (EN): Can I use my generator with solar?

A generator must not be connected in a way that sends power back into the grid or conflicts with the inverter's grid input. Ask a qualified installer to configure generator input, changeover, earthing, and protection correctly. Do not improvise wiring between a generator, UPS, inverter, and grid supply.
